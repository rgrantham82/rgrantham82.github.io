To the trade

Specifying original art for a client should be quick, documented, and free of ceremony.

Original acrylic paintings from an Austin studio, available directly to interior designers, architects, art consultants, and the people responsible for commercial walls. Trade pricing, works on approval, documented records, and a straight answer about whether a painting belongs in your space.

Terms at a glance

A tearsheet you can forward to a client without editing it first.

Trade discount
20% off published retail on original works, for qualified design professionals.
Volume placement
25% off published retail on placements of five or more works in a single project.
Qualification
Design or architecture firm, art consultancy, or corporate buyer. A Texas Sales and Use Tax Resale Certificate (Form 01-339) is accepted for resale purchases.
Holds
Ten business days at no charge while a client decides. Longer holds by arrangement.
On approval
Up to two works for fourteen days within the Austin metro, delivered and collected by the studio.
Payment
Payment on delivery, or net 30 against a signed purchase order for established firms. Commissions require a 50% deposit.
Lead time
Available originals ship or deliver within one week. Commissions run six to ten weeks depending on scale.
Returns
Fourteen days on trade purchases, in original condition, refund less return freight.

What arrives with every placement

  • The signed original, sealed with archival matte varnish and wired to hang
  • A certificate of authenticity and the studio inventory number
  • A documented record of title, year, medium, dimensions, depth, and finish
  • Condition confirmation and written care and cleaning notes for facilities staff
  • High-resolution photography for your client presentation, on request

Where the work goes

Design & architecture

Residential and hospitality projects

Originals sourced to a palette, a dimension, and a deadline. Send the wall photograph and its width and you will get a scaled mockup rather than an invitation to imagine.

Professional offices

Law, finance, and consulting

Lobbies, conference rooms, and partner offices, where the work has to hold a large wall and survive years of being looked at by people who are waiting. Larger Conjunctions pieces are built for exactly this.

Healthcare & wellness

Clinics and therapeutic spaces

Not every painting suits every room. Work for clinical and behavioral-health settings is chosen for steady color and open composition — nothing that asks a hard question of someone having a hard day. The studio will say plainly when a piece is wrong for a space, which is considerably cheaper than discovering it after installation.

How a project runs

  1. Step one

    Register and describe the project

    Firm, contact, project type, and resale certificate if you have one. Registration takes one email and does not commit you to anything.

  2. Step two

    Receive a curated selection

    A short list of available originals matched to your dimensions, palette, and budget, with trade pricing shown alongside retail. Six good options beat sixty.

  3. Step three

    Mockup, hold, or trial

    Scaled room mockups for client approval, a ten-day hold while they decide, or delivery on approval for Austin projects. Mockups are planning aids, not prophecies: screen color and room light are different animals.

  4. Step four

    Delivery and installation

    Studio delivery and hanging within the Austin metro. Insured freight beyond it, quoted by work and destination.

Commissions and scale

When nothing available fits the wall, the wall gets its own painting. Commissioned work begins with dimensions, the character of the room, a working budget, and an honest conversation about what the painting has to accomplish. Palette can be coordinated to a specification without the result becoming furniture.
