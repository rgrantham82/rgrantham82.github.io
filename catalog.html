What the catalog contains

Every plate documented

Title, year, medium, finish, dimensions in inches and centimeters, orientation, inventory number, and availability, consistent from the plate to the closing checklist.

Four sections

The work grouped as it is made: two ongoing series, the paintings that sit outside them, and the lettering the practice grew from.

Collecting terms

One price per size, the same at every venue, plus commissions, trade and corporate inquiries, and how the paintings are finished and cared for.
